# 实际操作演示

从数百篇论文的研究库中找到一篇论文，再沿着它的联系继续阅读。下面是一次本地 Obsidian 实际操作的连续演示，配有中英文步骤提示。

## 看什么

1. **整库浏览**：用方向树组织研究领域，在图上看见论文之间的联系。
2. **论文聚焦**：选择论文，查看它附近的关联；悬停时突出连接，便于追踪。
3. **关系阅读**：详情卡按类型列出关联论文，区分改进、复用、竞争与结论分歧。
4. **阅读属性**：在同一张卡片里查看论文简介、阅读进度、重要度和标签。
5. **沿图探索**：切换论文，比较不同论文的局部关系网络。
6. **跨方向导航**：回到目录树，进入另一个研究领域。

图中的连线来自知识库中已有的笔记与关系记录。录屏展示浏览与查看属性；添加论文、修改正文与关系编辑的完整步骤见 [使用指南](guide.md)。需要 AI 帮忙补全笔记时，先配置 [AI 协作入口](guide.md#与-ai-协作)，然后使用 `ingest` 约定。

演示由用户提供的原始录屏剪辑而成，裁去黑边、压缩重复操作与等待，并移除链接提示弹窗片段；没有重绘产品界面。示例论文、PDF 与研究正文不随安装包分发，新库从空库骨架开始。

## English walkthrough

The animation above is a continuous demo edited from a real local Obsidian research session, with bilingual captions. [Open the MP4 version](pinakes-demo.mp4) for clearer playback and pause controls.

1. **Browse the library.** Use the topic tree to organize research areas and the graph to see connections.
2. **Focus on a paper.** Select a paper and highlight its neighboring connections by hovering.
3. **Follow typed relationships.** The detail card groups related papers by extensions, reuse, competition, and conflicting conclusions.
4. **Inspect reading properties.** View the summary, reading status, importance, and tags together.
5. **Explore paper neighborhoods.** Switch papers to compare their local relationship networks.
6. **Switch research topics.** Return to the tree and enter another field.

This recording demonstrates browsing and inspecting existing information. Importing papers, editing notes, and changing relationships are covered in the [user guide (Chinese)](guide.md).

The edit removes black borders, repeated actions, waiting, and link-notification segments, without redrawing the interface. Example papers, PDFs, and research notes are not bundled with the installer. New libraries start with a minimal structure.
